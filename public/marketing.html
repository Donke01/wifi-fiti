COMMON QUESTIONS

What operators usually want to know.

Do I need a public IP address for my MikroTik?

No. The router checks in to WiFi Fiti over outbound HTTPS, so you do not need to expose WinBox or the RouterOS API publicly. You still need administrator access to configure the router and a working MikroTik Hotspot.

Which routers does this setup support?

The current pairing flow is built for MikroTik RouterOS Hotspot. The router needs internet access, DNS, HTTPS fetch permission and a configured customer bridge and Hotspot server.

Will a customer’s time pause when they disconnect?

No. A confirmed package uses a server-side wall-clock expiry. The remaining time continues whether the customer is online or offline, and refreshing reads that same stored expiry.

Can a business use its own Till or PayBill?

Yes. A business can connect verified Daraja credentials for its own collection setup. It can also choose WiFi Fiti collection, which applies the displayed platform fee to completed customer sales.

Are payouts sent automatically?

No. The platform records and reserves payout requests for manual review. It does not automatically transfer money, verify bank disbursements, or handle refunds and chargebacks.
