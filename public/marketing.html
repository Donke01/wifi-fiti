COMMON QUESTIONS

What operators need to know.

Do I need a public IP address?

No. Your router polls WiFi Fiti over HTTPS, so WinBox and the RouterOS API stay private.

Which routers work?

MikroTik RouterOS Hotspot. The router needs internet, DNS, HTTPS fetch permission, a customer bridge, and a Hotspot server.

Does time pause when a customer disconnects?

No. Time continues from the payment-confirmed expiry, online or offline.

Can I use my own Till or PayBill?

Yes. Connect verified Daraja credentials, or use WiFi Fiti collection at 5% of each confirmed sale.

When WiFi Fiti collects payments, are payouts automatic?

No. Payout requests are reviewed before payment. WiFi Fiti does not send money automatically.
