Remote access

Lets Wi-Fi Fiti reach a router privately to finish or repair its setup. Customer traffic and payments never use this path.

It turns on by itself once a new router finishes setup. You can turn it off for any router below at any time, and it stays off until you turn it on again. Wi-Fi Fiti never exposes WinBox, API, or SSH to the public internet.

Router tools

Check and look after a router without opening it: health, log, a speed test, one customer’s connection, a backup, a restart, or a live terminal. Each runs through the router’s own secure check-in.

Packages

Create the time plans customers buy on your portals. The same packages back your voucher batches. Speed caps are enforced by the router and never shown to customers.

Choose days, week, or month instead of converting to hours. A cap such as 2M/5M is applied on the router only.
